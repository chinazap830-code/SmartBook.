About offline use

SmartBook. stores your books and practice progress in this browser. After the first successful visit while online, the app shell is cached for offline opening. This version supports TXT and Markdown text; scanned PDFs, images and Word documents are not yet supported. It does not use paid APIs or send your books to a server.
